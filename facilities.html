Our Facilities

State-of-the-art medical infrastructure designed for the highest standard of patient care.

Surgery

Operating Theatres

Our advanced operating theatres are equipped with the latest surgical technology for a wide range of procedures, ensuring patient safety and optimal outcomes. Staffed by experienced surgical teams available 24/7.

Imaging

MRI & CT Scan

High-resolution MRI and CT scan machines for accurate and detailed diagnostic imaging. Our radiology team provides rapid results crucial for precise medical assessments and treatment planning.

Diagnostics

Modern Laboratory

A fully equipped laboratory offering a comprehensive range of diagnostic tests — from routine blood work to specialized pathology — with rapid and reliable results to support clinical decision-making.

Critical Care

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Our ICU provides critical care services for severely ill patients, staffed by highly skilled professionals and supported by advanced monitoring systems and life-support technology around the clock.

Emergency

Emergency Department

24/7 emergency services with a dedicated team prepared to handle all types of medical emergencies efficiently and effectively. Ambulance services available. Call: +251 920 008 338.

Pharmacy

Hospital Pharmacy

A well-stocked pharmacy providing a wide range of medications, managed by qualified pharmacists to ensure safe and appropriate drug dispensation for all inpatients and outpatients.

Inpatient

Comfortable Patient Rooms

Spacious and comfortable patient rooms designed to provide a restful and healing environment, equipped with modern amenities for patient well-being and family comfort during recovery.

Imaging

Ultrasound & X-ray

Advanced ultrasound and X-ray facilities for non-invasive diagnostic imaging, essential for various medical assessments including obstetrics, cardiology, and musculoskeletal conditions.
